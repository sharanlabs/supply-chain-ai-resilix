Responsible Roles: Immediate: Crisis Response Team, Supplier Relations|Short-term: VP Supply Chain, HR (employee safety)|Strategic: CEO, Board Risk Committee
Escalation Criteria: Escalate to C-suite if: any supplier facility is confirmed destroyed or severely damaged, recovery timeline exceeds 60 days, revenue exposure exceeds $5M, humanitarian concerns require corporate response, or product availability drops below 70% of demand

Immediate Actions:
1. Confirm safety and status of personnel at affected supplier facilities
2. Assess structural and operational damage to supplier manufacturing sites
3. Identify all products and components sourced from affected area
4. Check current inventory levels for affected product lines
5. Determine transportation infrastructure status (roads, ports, airports)
6. Activate business continuity contacts at affected suppliers

Short-term Actions:
1. Engage backup suppliers to fill production gap during recovery
2. Assess supplier recovery timeline based on damage severity
3. Prioritize shipment of available finished goods from unaffected facilities
4. Coordinate with logistics providers on alternate transport routes
5. Evaluate expedited air freight for critical shortage items
6. Support affected suppliers with advance payments or flexible terms if appropriate

Strategic Actions:
1. Require supplier business continuity plans as part of qualification criteria
2. Map geographic concentration risk across full supplier base
3. Establish regional emergency response partnerships
4. Build parametric insurance coverage for high-risk geographic zones
5. Diversify manufacturing across multiple geographic regions
6. Invest in predictive natural disaster monitoring for supplier locations
