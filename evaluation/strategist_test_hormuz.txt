Description: Response framework for shipping route disruption caused by military conflict in a maritime chokepoint region
Severity Threshold: 4
Estimated Recovery Days: 30-180
Source Frameworks: ISO 22301 Business Continuity|NIST SP 800-34 Contingency Planning|BIMCO Maritime Security Guidelines
Responsible Roles: Immediate: Crisis Response Team, Procurement Lead|Short-term: VP Supply Chain, Logistics Director|Strategic: Chief Supply Chain Officer, Board Risk Committee
Escalation Criteria: Escalate to C-suite if: revenue exposure exceeds $5M, inventory coverage falls below 14 days for any CRITICAL product, or disruption is projected to last beyond 30 days

Immediate Actions:
1. Activate crisis response team and notify executive leadership
2. Conduct emergency inventory audit for all products sourced through affected chokepoint
3. Contact all suppliers in affected region to confirm operational status
4. Place hold on new purchase orders routed through disrupted corridor
5. Assess current safety stock levels against projected demand for next 30 days
6. Identify vessels currently in transit through or near the affected zone

Short-term Actions:
1. Engage backup suppliers and request expedited capacity allocation
2. Evaluate alternate shipping routes with cost and transit time comparison
3. Adjust safety stock targets upward for affected product lines
4. Negotiate emergency freight rates with carriers on alternate routes
5. Review insurance coverage for war risk and business interruption
6. Brief sales and customer service teams on potential delivery delays

Strategic Actions:
1. Diversify supplier base to reduce concentration in single chokepoint corridor
2. Establish long-term contracts with carriers on multiple route options
3. Evaluate nearshoring or regional sourcing for critical single-source items
4. Build strategic buffer inventory for highest-criticality products
5. Develop permanent dual-routing capability for top revenue product lines
6. Review and update business continuity plan with lessons learned
